Wahoo Bay design system

Tokens and components for wahoobay.net and the fish-ID app. A white ground, a pale blue wash, one sans-serif and 12px corners. Brand blue is the logo, the large headings and the large numbers, action blue is what you press, and the Seagrass accent means alive right now.

Components

On the light ground, which is every page except the live cameras. The navigation bar above and the footer below are components too.

Lead and hidden text

A lead paragraph opens a page under its heading. Words only a screen reader needs go in wb-sr-only, and every page starts with a wb-skip link that appears when Tab reaches it.

Wahoo Bay is a public marine education park at the Hillsboro Inlet, inspiring ocean literacy through hands-on learning, immersive field experiences, and community conservation.

A screen reader hears one more sentence here than the page shows. This is it: text in wb-sr-only is read aloud and never drawn. Press Tab from the top of the page to see the skip link.

Icons and the back link

One SVG set in icons/, drawn with one 2px stroke in the text colour. Inline the file: it is already marked decorative, so the words beside it name the link. The back link leads up to the page a page belongs to, with the gap above the heading larger than the one below it.

Buttons

Action blue is the primary action, ghost the secondary, and the accent is Donate and nothing else. Every button is at least 44px tall.

Watch the reef live Plan a visit Donate

The last button is drawn as keyboard focus draws it: the ring comes from base.css, 2px of --wb-color-focus set 2px clear of the button. Press Tab to see it on the others.

A button that cannot be pressed is disabled (or aria-disabled="true"): it fades and does not answer the pointer. Its text falls below 4.5:1 on purpose, as WCAG 1.4.3 exempts an inactive control; say beside it why it is off. A button at work adds wb-btn--busy while it is disabled: its label stays and a ring turns after it, standing still for a reader who asks for reduced motion.

Document buttons

A button with a long label wraps instead of running off a narrow screen (wb-btn--wrap). What it opens goes last, in wb-btn__meta: muted on a ghost button, in the button's own colour on a filled one. The first document is the action; the rest are ghosts.

Captions

Small, in secondary ink: under a photo, or the text of a link to a post. A caption link is underlined only on hover and focus.

A manatee came in with the tide this morning, under the dock by the seawall.

Photos in running text

A photo, video or embed in wb-prose, the Markdown body of a page, has the 12px corners of the cards and panels around it, whether it stands in a paragraph or in a figure with its caption. A component inside prose keeps its own corners.

Wahoo Bay sits within the Hillsboro Inlet at Roy L. Rogers Family Park.

Banners and standalone photos

A photo or video outside running text, such as a page's banner or a post's photo, is a wb-media block: as wide as its column, with the 12px corners, at its own shape unless its context gives it one. In a band the photo is cropped about its centre; wb-media--top or wb-media--bottom keeps a subject at that edge in frame.

Map frame

The box an interactive map fills, or its still while the map loads: a 1px line and the 12px corners, as tall as most of the screen without filling it. The map's own words, its popups and buttons, are in the system's face. A place is a wb-marker, a pin in action blue ringed in the ground colour, in a 44px target; a camera's pin, wb-marker--camera, is the accent, because a camera is alive right now. Name each with aria-label.

Stage

The home page opens on a photograph as tall as most of the screen (about 820px at 1440, never taller than the viewport) with the headline on it at the display size. The scrim darkens the lower half of the picture, where the words sit, and a band at its top, deep enough for the three rows the navigation wraps onto on a phone, keeps it readable. The navigation lies over the top of it, transparent, with the logo and links in white: wb-nav--over. Text on a photograph is white in both themes, and a ghost button on it is outlined in white.
