Wahoo Bay design system

Tokens and components for wahoobay.net and the fish-ID app. A white ground, a pale blue wash, one sans-serif and 12px corners. Brand blue is the logo, the large headings and the large numbers, action blue is what you press, and the Seagrass accent means alive right now.

Every colour and size in the components is one of these custom properties, from tokens.css. Use the names; the values can change.

Components

On the light ground, which is every page except the live cameras. The navigation bar above and the footer below are components too.

Buttons

Action blue is the primary action, ghost the secondary, and the accent is Donate and nothing else. Every button is at least 44px tall.

The last button is drawn as keyboard focus draws it: the ring comes from base.css, 2px of --wb-color-focus set 2px clear of the button. Press Tab to see it on the others.
