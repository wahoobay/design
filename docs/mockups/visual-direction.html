Which direction feels like Wahoo Bay?

Three ways to dress the same home page. Each one uses the logo untouched and builds on Wahoo Bay blue; what changes is the type, the supporting colours and the mood. The bottom row of each shows the fish-ID dashboard in that direction, because continuity between the public pages and the data tools is the point.

Photos are from the current site. Headline, numbers and species are placeholders.

Field Guide

Warm and editorial, like a good park visitor centre. Sand background, serif headlines, soft corners, photos first. Says: a place, a community, education. Lightest touch for the data tools.
